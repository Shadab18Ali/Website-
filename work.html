01 / CLIENTLuxury storefront

Diviniti

Platform
Shopify 2.0
Problem
A luxury catalogue of 500+ products with slow-loading images, little flexibility for merchandising changes and limited insight into how customers used the site.
My role
Shopify development, Liquid customisation, responsive implementation and performance optimisation.
What I did
Rebuilt the image-loading architecture, created 20+ editable Shopify Liquid sections and improved catalogue usability. Set up Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity for tracking.
Result
40% reported improvement in Largest Contentful Paint.

02 / CLIENTMade-to-order store

Portrait on Gold

Platform
Shopify 2.0
Problem
Customers needed to choose a portrait’s size, frame and finish, and the business needed to confirm cash-on-delivery orders before producing anything personalised.
My role
Shopify development: collection filtering in JavaScript, product-option logic in Liquid and the order verification flow.
What I did
Built app-free collection filtering, Liquid logic for size, frame and finish combinations, and a WhatsApp-first order check through WATI.
Result
Orders are confirmed on WhatsApp before a custom portrait goes into production, and filtering works without a paid filter app.
  • Shopify Development
  • Liquid
  • JavaScript
  • WATI Integration

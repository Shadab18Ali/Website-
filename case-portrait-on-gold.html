The context

What needed to work.

The problem, the part I owned, and the decision that shaped the build.

The brief

Customers needed to choose portrait options such as size, frame, and finish. The business also needed a way to confirm cash-on-delivery intent before producing a personalized order.

My role

I implemented collection filtering in JavaScript, product-option logic in Liquid, and the WhatsApp-first verification flow through WATI. The storefront was built mobile-first.

The key decision

Custom filtering avoided reliance on an additional paid filter app. The verification step put a decision point before production, when an unconfirmed made-to-order request could create avoidable cost.

Implementation

How the build came together.

01 / 03

Browse without extra app weight

Built JavaScript-based collection filtering rather than adding a paid Shopify filter app.

02 / 03

Handle product complexity

Used Liquid logic to present the combinations of portrait size, frame, and finish.

03 / 03

Confirm before fulfillment

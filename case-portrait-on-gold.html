Portrait on Gold

A made-to-order portrait store on Shopify, with custom product options, app-free filtering and a WhatsApp-first order check.

View live site ↗ (opens in a new tab)
Client
Portrait on Gold
Platform
Shopify
Industry
Made-to-order portraits
Role
Shopify development, Liquid, JavaScript, WATI integration

/Details

Challenge

Customers needed to choose portrait options such as size, frame and finish.

The business also needed to confirm cash-on-delivery orders before producing a personalised portrait, and wanted collection filtering without relying on a paid filter app.

Solution

I implemented the product options in Liquid, the collection filtering in JavaScript, and a WhatsApp-first order verification flow through WATI.

Implementation

  • Custom product variant logic in Liquid for size, frame and finish
  • App-free collection filtering in JavaScript
  • WhatsApp-first order verification through WATI

Results

Orders are confirmed on WhatsApp before a personalised portrait goes into production.

Collections can be filtered without a paid filter app.

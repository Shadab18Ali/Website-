Project 02 · Shopify / Ecommerce Development

Portrait on Gold

A made-to-order portrait store on Shopify, with custom product options, app-free filtering and a WhatsApp-first order check.

Client
Portrait on Gold
Platform
Shopify 2.0
Industry
Made-to-order portraits
Role
Shopify development, Liquid, JavaScript, WATI integration

/Details

Challenge

Every portrait is made to order, so customers needed to choose size, frame and finish without confusion.

The business also needed to confirm cash-on-delivery orders before producing anything personalised, and wanted fast collection filtering without adding a paid filter app.

Solution

I built the product logic and ordering flow around how the business produces its portraits.

  • Custom product variant logic in Liquid for portrait size, frame and finish combinations
  • App-free collection filtering written in JavaScript
  • A WhatsApp-first order verification flow through WATI, so orders are confirmed before production

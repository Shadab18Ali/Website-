Good to know

A few things you might be wondering.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Redesigns and improvements are a service of their own: Shopify customisation, bug fixes, speed improvements, and changes to existing WordPress and WooCommerce sites. Share the URL and what you want to improve.

What do you need to start?

A short description of your business, what you need the site to do, and any timeline you have in mind. A current URL, brand assets, or a design are useful if you have them, but you can start the conversation without them.

Can we discuss a custom feature?

Absolutely. Describe the workflow or problem in the project form. I’ll review the scope with you and suggest a practical way to build it.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. Post-launch support is part of the process. We’ll confirm the specific scope and duration for your project before work begins.

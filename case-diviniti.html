Project 01 · Shopify Development

Diviniti

A luxury Shopify storefront with a catalogue of more than 500 products, rebuilt around faster image loading and sections the team can edit themselves.

Client
Diviniti
Platform
Shopify 2.0
Industry
Luxury ecommerce
Role
Shopify development, Liquid customisation, responsive development, performance optimisation

/Details

Challenge

Managing a large ecommerce catalogue while maintaining a clean browsing experience and improving storefront performance.

With more than 500 products, images loaded slowly, merchandising changes were hard to make, and the team needed clearer insight into how customers used the site.

Solution

I worked on the Shopify implementation, developed reusable Liquid sections, improved responsive behaviour and optimised performance areas including image loading.

  • Responsive image sizes and lazy-loading rules, with separate choices for mobile and desktop
  • 20+ Liquid sections with schemas, so the team can edit and reorder page content in the Shopify editor
  • Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity for tracking, plus structured on-page SEO across the catalogue
  • 500+Products in the catalogue
  • 20+Editable Liquid sections
  • 40%Reported improvement in Largest Contentful Paint

/Image gallery

Merchandising layouts are built from editable Liquid sections, so content can be rearranged in the Shopify editor without code changes.
